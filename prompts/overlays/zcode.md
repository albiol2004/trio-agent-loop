# zcode — native ZCode skills (embedded only)

ZCode has NO standalone role files in-repo: `zcode/skills/trio/SKILL.md`
carries the orchestration prose and `zcode/skills/trio-init/SKILL.md` the
initializer. The role protocol reaches this flavor through the marked-region
upsert of the protocol-essentials block into `zcode/skills/trio/SKILL.md`
(see the EMBEDDED table in generate.py). No role targets.
