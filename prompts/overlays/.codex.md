# .codex — project-local Codex agent mirror

The `.codex/agents/` tree is the project-local mirror of the native Codex
agents. It must stay byte-identical to the `codex` flavor's native toml
outputs (install.sh copies `codex/agents/` into `~/.codex/agents/`); it has no
fallback-prompt surface of its own.

inherits: codex
